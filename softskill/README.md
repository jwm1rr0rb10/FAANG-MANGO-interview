## Pro-Tips for Delivery:
**Listen actively to the interviewer's answers. Ask a brief, thoughtful follow-up.**

**Tailor your questions. If the interviewer mentioned a specific project, ask about that.**

**Have 5-7 questions prepared. You might not need all of them.**

**End strong: "Based on our conversation, I'm even more excited about this opportunity. I believe my experience in X aligns well with your need for Y. What are the next steps?"**
